## 1. Printed output

- **Hollow sections get a spiral and a Fermat curve (D1).** `Spiral.NestedChain` decides
  whether rings nest by containment instead of bounding boxes, so an outer skin with a hole
  skin inside it (an annulus, a vase with an inner wall) is one nested chain again: the
  annulus fixture went from 6 independent rings to 1 spiral. An L-shaped notch that the old
  bounding-box test called nested is pinned as a false positive that stays refused. This
  closes the "hollow sections still get no spiral" item of the 4.3.0 notes.
- **Every offset ring has a bounded vertex count (D2).** Clipper's round joins tessellate
  each convex corner at its 0.1 um working precision and an iterated inward offset inherits
  the previous ring's vertices, so hole-side rings compounded: 81, 481, 1117, 2090 vertices
  over three iterations on an r40/r20 annulus at spacing 3, while every downstream pass
  (spiral blend, non-planar resampling, deviation index, G-code, preview) paid for them.
  `Offset.Polygons` now thins each ring with RDP at `min(0.02, |delta| / 200)` mm; all four
  hole-side rings come out at 81 and no thinned vertex sits more than 0.0096 mm off the raw
  ring. The skirt offset is thinned by the same rule. Feeding Clipper's arc tolerance through
  the reflection shim was measured and rejected (it only slows the growth to 2n-1 per
  iteration).
- **Bricklayer works on round parts (D10, found by D7's pipeline pin).** Wall loops arrive
  from Clipper's integer grid and the new thinning, so on a cylinder, cone or vase the
  measured wall-to-wall gap lands a few micrometres outside the nominal spacing. The
  adjacency gate's epsilon was relative (1e-9), so no two loops on a round part counted as
  adjacent and the pass reported "0 wall loop(s) raised" - the hand-built rectangle fixtures
  never caught it. `Bricklayer.AdjacencyGate` now tests at `bead + max(1e-3, 2% of bead)`;
  the 48-gon cylinder fixture goes from 0 of 4 eligible layers raised to 4 of 4, the
  five-wall pipeline cylinder from 6 of 7 to 7 of 7. The overlap-fraction gate is untouched.
  The cone tip's odd-cycle guard still leaves some layers flat (known, listed under Owed).
- **Non-planar output is thinned to what the wave bends (D9).** Modulated infill and wall
  paths were emitted at the resampling density even where the wave is flat; `Rdp.SimplifyIndices`
  now thins them after modulation at `min(0.02, amplitude / 50)` while keeping the seam
  index. A 200 mm wave line went from 130 to 10 points; the shape is unchanged within the
  tolerance.

## 2. Correctness

- **The axis glitch filter no longer mutates the published poll (D3).** `AxisGlitchFilter`
  returns a filtered view (`ObjectModel.WithPositionRepair`, `GlitchResolution.RawSnapshot`
  and `WasCorrected`) instead of overwriting the machine position inside the snapshot every
  subscriber sees. The decision logic is unchanged and the dome replay tests pin it; the
  raw poll is preserved for the log.
- **Core output is culture-invariant (D4).** 63 formatting and parsing sites that used the
  current culture are fixed: DoE report and export text, Duet file paths and jog G-code,
  print-stats report and save-path checks, interlayer-time report and markers, the Fermat
  ring dedup key, the pump-calibration comment check. Eight de-DE guard tests run the
  affected paths under a comma-decimal culture. The rule is now an ARCHI section 5
  principle and CA1305/CA1310/CA1309 are build warnings (section 5 below).
- **No silent catch in Core (D6a).** 80 catch sites classified: typed to the exceptions the
  path can raise, or a justified swallow with the reason in place; `OperationCanceledException`
  guards added where a cancelled task was being reported as a failure.
- **No silent component failure in the plugin (D6b).** 72 plugin catch sites typed and
  justified; four failures the operator could not notice now say what happened, with the
  fallback unchanged: Graph Mapper raises an archive notice when a saved curve cannot be read
  (the notice survives the solve that clears ordinary messages); Legend warns when a wired
  Gradient's grips cannot be read instead of treating it as no gradient; Desirability and RSM
  Evaluate remark when their canvas panel fails to draw; the Machine Console reports a job log
  it could not close. Test sheet section 1f (SILENT-01..05).
- **Two review findings on the round itself.** The shared `Num.Clamp01` from H1 read NaN
  as 0 while three of the four private copies it replaced let NaN through; it propagates
  NaN again and the print console guards its job fraction itself. The diagnostics D7 moved
  into `SlicePipeline` (adaptive layer heights, conical clearance, bricklayer shift,
  non-planar amplitudes, skipped-layer z) and the two non-planar amplitude refusals
  formatted doubles with the current culture; wrapped in `FormattableString.Invariant`
  and pinned by a de-DE slice comparison.
- **Analyzer-found mistakes (H4).** Wrong `paramName` in 80 `ArgumentException` sites,
  missing `==`/`!=` and comparison operators on structs that override `Equals`, cancellation
  tokens not forwarded through response reads and `Task.Run`, `IndexOf` used for membership
  tests, an XML reader created without DTD processing disabled.

## 3. Structure

- **The slicer's orchestration lives in Core (D7).** `SlicePipeline.Slice(vertices, faces,
  options)` / `SliceFromPerimeters` with `SliceOptions`, `LevelPlan` and `SliceResult`; the
  Slice component only converts Rhino geometry and builds the tree. Pinned by a golden hash
  over three meshes before and after the move (23 tests), which is how D10 was found.
- **Two more plugin decisions moved into Core (D8).** `ActionGatePolicy` (the machine
  action-gate set and its severities) and `SettingsPlan` (classify a settings file and plan
  its application); 36 tests; two coverage-debt rows shrunk.
- **One home for the small numeric helpers (H1).** `Num.Finite/Clamp/Clamp01` and
  `PolylineOps.Dist2/Dist2Xy` replace about twenty private copies across Core and the plugin,
  one file per commit.
- **`LayerBuilder` takes one options record (H2).** `LayerBuildOptions` replaces eight
  positional overloads at twenty call sites.
- **Shared infill tail (H3).** `Infill.FinishSpans` finishes the zigzag and wave spans once.
- **Dead public API (H8).** Five unreferenced methods deleted (`Spiral.Inward`,
  `Spiral.Paths`, three `RecoveryReducer` transitions no caller used), fifteen public helpers
  narrowed to private or internal so Core's public surface matches what the plugin and tests
  call.

### Component text pass (2026-09-15)

Every component description, input and output description, context-menu tooltip, runtime
message (warning, error, remark, archive notice) and machine action result was rewritten
in one house style: US English, sentence case, one idea per sentence, units after a
trailing comma (`Layer height, mm`), special values as `(0 = off)`, ranges as `0 to 1`,
no semicolons and no dashes as separators (fixed terms such as G-code, non-planar and
E-stop keep their hyphen), no shouting capitals, and identical wording for the sockets
shared across components (`host`, `reachMax`, `reachMin`, `elbow`, `robot`, `bed`,
`progress`, `log`, the password note). Runtime messages open with the problem and end
with what to do. Socket names, nicknames, wire hints, GUIDs and Core message wording are
untouched, so saved documents and the test-pinned Core texts are unaffected. The wiki
socket tables were regenerated and merged into the hand-maintained pages
(`Generate-ComponentPages.ps1` still carries pre-4.1.0 prose and drops a sidebar link, so
its output is not taken verbatim).

### Live View follows the intended path (2026-09-16)

The live arm no longer animates along chords between reported positions. It presumes the
machine executes its file: a reading on the intended path is progress (arc-length
parametrized `LivePathDeviation`, new `Core/Duet/LivePathFollower.cs`), the arm is posed
on the path there and moves along the file's own route between readings, with a display
delay of two poll intervals. A reading more than 5 mm off the path is held instead of
hiding the arm (the seam of every layer produced two such readings and the arm vanished
each time); an advance the recent rate cannot explain is deferred until the next reading
corroborates it (the firmware reports a queued move's target for one poll, which was the
spike on every travel); only after 1.5 s off the path is the measured position shown, or
the progress relocated when the machine is elsewhere on the path. A transport retry holds
the last pose instead of hiding it. The trail, the outputs and the logs are untouched and
keep every reading. The `arm:` line on `info` reports the source of the pose and the held,
deferred, dropped, deviating and relocated counts. Replay fixtures from the two 2026-09-16
captures pin the behaviour (`LivePathFollowerTests`). Also removed the leftover merge
markers in `GcodeRestartComponent`.
